
Keyboard Shortcuts

• Jump to a specific desktop: Press Alt + F1 for the first desktop, Alt + F2 for the second, and so on.
• Cycle left or right: Press Ctrl + Alt + Left Arrow or Ctrl + Alt + Right :Arrow.

Default Window Shortcuts

You can use Moksha's built-in shortcuts immediately without changing settings:
• Window Menu (Minimize/Maximize/Close). Press Ctrl+Alt+W to open the active window's options menu.
• Minimize Window. Press Ctrl+Alt+I (Iconic Mode Toggle).
• Maximize Window. Press Super (Windows) + Up or Ctrl+Alt+N.
